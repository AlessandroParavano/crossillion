    How to play

    Each round gives you 7 prompts and 25 seconds each (after a 3-second preview). Type one valid answer.

    Common answers keep you near the surface. Rare answers carry you down through the street, the nave, the crypt and the catacombs toward the Holy of Holies. Seven perfect answers reach 7,000 meters. Zones begin at 0, 200, 1,000, 4,000 and 6,000 m.

    Close, but not perfect? You'll be offered a correction. Press Enter again to accept it.

    • Flock 100 m
    • Pharisee 150 m
    • Sunday Schooler 300 m
    • Seminarian 600 m
    • Theologian 850 m
    • Burning Bush 1,000 m

    How rarity is measured. Every answer in the game was ranked by how many real people looked it up on English Wikipedia over a full 12-month window (human traffic only, bots excluded). Within each prompt, the most-looked-up answers are the commonest tier and the least-looked-up are the rarest. See the README for details.

    A new Descent unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
