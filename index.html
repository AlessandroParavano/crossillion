    How to play

    Each round gives you 7 questions and 25 seconds each (after a 3-second preview). Type one valid answer.

    Common answers keep you near the surface. Rare answers carry you down through the street, the nave, the crypt and the catacombs toward the Holy of Holies. Perfect answers reach 7,000 meters. Zones begin at m.

    Some questions add a rule, like a starting letter or a time period. Answers that break the rule don't count.

    Close, but not perfect? You'll be offered a correction. Press Enter again to accept it.

      How rarity is measured. Every answer in the game was ranked by how many real people looked it up on English Wikipedia over a full 12-month window (human traffic only, bots excluded). Within each question, the most-looked-up answers are the commonest tier and the least-looked-up are the rarest. See the README for details.

      The speaker and note buttons at the top turn sound effects and music on or off. A new Descent unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
